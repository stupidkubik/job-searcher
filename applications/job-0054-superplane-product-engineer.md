It saved about three minutes per screenshot, typically 15–20 screenshots on a complex article, and more than 10 hours of manual work per month. The bigger result was a more predictable production process with less repetitive handling. I owned the problem from identifying the bottleneck through implementation and adoption, which is the kind of end-to-end product work I enjoy most.
